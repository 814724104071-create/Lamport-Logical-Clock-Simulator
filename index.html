How it works

  1. Each process maintains its own logical clock.
  2. Before every event, the process increments its clock by 1.
  3. When sending a message, the sender attaches its clock value.
  4. When receiving a message, the receiver sets its clock to the maximum of its own clock and the received timestamp, then adds 1.
